How to Use RailAgent & Important Rules

Just Chat Naturally

You don't need to know complex station codes or train numbers. Just talk to the AI like a travel agent! Type things like "Find trains to Ernakulam for tomorrow" or "Track sleeper seats for Friday." The AI will figure out the rest.

Your Digital Assistant

RailAgent works by opening a hidden browser on your computer. It physically clicks buttons, types in your saved passenger details, and navigates the IRCTC website automatically, exactly like a human would do it.

Silent Tracking

If tickets are waitlisted, ask the agent to "Track" the train. It will silently check the IRCTC website in the background every few minutes. You can minimize the window, and it will alert you as soon as a seat becomes available!

You Handle the Money

For your absolute safety, RailAgent cannot and will not make payments. It will do all the boring work of filling out passenger forms, but it will always pause at the payment screen so you can securely enter your own UPI or card details.

Risk of Account Suspension

IRCTC has strict rules against automated booking bots. While RailAgent tries to act like a human, searching or booking too fast, too many times a day can result in IRCTC temporarily suspending your account. Please use this tool responsibly for your personal travel only.
